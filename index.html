How it works

F.O.R.G.E. (Flexible Operations & Robotics Guided Environment) started as a CSULB senior design project: a smart workbench on a Raspberry Pi 5 that detected tools with YOLOv11, pointed at them with a laser on a pan-tilt mount, and took voice commands through Vosk.

This version runs the whole pipeline on a phone, in the browser. Nothing is uploaded: every camera frame is processed on the device.
